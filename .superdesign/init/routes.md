## Product taxonomy

- Assess: Checker, Match, Fixer, Parser X-Ray.
- Create: Builder, Maker, Templates, Cover Letter.
- Prepare: Interview, LinkedIn, Salary, Guides.
- Organize/apply: Jobs, Tracker, Daily Matches, approved Auto Apply.

Dynamic/public SEO families include roles, companies, countries, blog posts and comparisons. Admin, payment, account, queue and support routes require authenticated state and are not design-source targets for the initial public-core design work.
