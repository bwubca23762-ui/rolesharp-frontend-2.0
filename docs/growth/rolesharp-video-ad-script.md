# RoleSharp 30-Second Video Ad

## Campaign goal

Create a high-retention vertical video that introduces RoleSharp to students, new graduates, and active job seekers. The ad should make one promise clear: RoleSharp helps a candidate turn a generic resume into a job-specific, ATS-ready application before they apply.

Primary CTA: **Build your job-ready resume at RoleSharp.com**

Student CTA: **Verify your university email and get 6 months of Student Premium free. No card required.**

## Best format for traffic

- Duration: 30 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920
- Platforms: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn video
- Safe layout: keep captions and important UI inside the center 80% of the frame
- Editing pace: visual change every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds
- Captions: always burned in; many viewers watch without sound
- Character: one relatable female university student or recent graduate, age 21-25
- Setting: bright, realistic bedroom/study desk, then a confident interview-ready final shot
- Visual identity: RoleSharp navy `#1e3a5f`, blue `#2563eb`, green `#059669`, white, and ink `#0f172a`
- Typography: Poppins-style bold headings and clean Open Sans-style captions
- Tone: practical, hopeful, modern, credible; no exaggerated promises

## Core creative idea

**One job. One generic resume. One sharper application.**

The story follows a student who finds a role she really wants but sees that her resume is weak and generic. RoleSharp checks the match, identifies missing keywords, improves truthful bullet points, and creates the supporting application materials. The student offer becomes the final reason to act now.

## Voice and performance direction

- Voice: female, early-to-mid 20s, warm, clear, confident, conversational
- Accent: globally understandable Indian English or neutral international English
- Pace: approximately 150-165 words per minute
- Delivery: begin with genuine frustration, gain momentum during the product reveal, and land the student offer clearly
- Emphasize: **exact role**, **six months free**, **no card**, and **RoleSharp.com**
- Do not sound like a loud sales announcer

## Editing notes

- Open with the problem in the first second; do not begin with the logo
- Show the product by second four
- Make the ATS score movement visual but do not imply a guaranteed score or interview
- Use real RoleSharp screen recordings over generated UI whenever possible
- Keep the student offer visible for at least four seconds
- Show `RoleSharp.com` for the full final three seconds
- Add a small footer during the offer: `Eligibility and fair-use limits apply`
- Export one master without platform watermarks
