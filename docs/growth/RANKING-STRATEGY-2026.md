# RoleSharp Ranking Strategy 2026

Written: 2026-07-08

This is the working SEO and distribution plan for RoleSharp based on:

- the current site architecture in `app/`
- the current product surfaces already live
- verified public contributor pages for outreach
- current SERP patterns for resume builder, ATS checker, and ATS template queries

The goal is not "more content". The goal is:

1. win keywords that already match RoleSharp's strongest product flows
2. avoid fighting giant brands head-on where they already dominate
3. build authority through the few topics where product, content, and outreach all align

## Executive Summary

RoleSharp should rank best by focusing on four keyword systems:

1. ATS checker and ATS-friendly resume content
2. student resume / internship / early-career content
3. role-specific resume example and template content
4. country-aware job-search and resume adaptation content

### Highest-probability wins

- `free resume builder for students`
- `student resume builder`
- `ats resume templates`
- `resume examples by role`
- `ats friendly resume`
- `resume for [role]`
- `jobs in [country]` plus "resume for [country]" adjacency

### Harder and slower keywords

- `ai resume builder`
- `free ats resume checker`
- `best ai resume builder`

These are still worth pursuing, but they are crowded by brands with larger link profiles and more direct tool equity.

## RoleSharp's Current Weaknesses

### 1. Keyword cannibalization risk

There are several near-overlapping surfaces:

- `/checker` vs `/ats-resume-checker`
- `/build` vs `/resume-builder`
- `/resume-maker` vs `/resume-templates`

This is not fatal, but it needs deliberate positioning.

### 2. Homepage is broad, not sharply keyword-owned

The homepage is good brand copy, but it is not trying to own one core commercial keyword with the same aggression as large competitors.

That is acceptable if subpages do the ranking work, but it means homepage rank growth will depend more on authority than on pure on-page targeting.

### 3. Jobs is useful, but member-only search limits pure job-board behavior

`/jobs` can still rank as a landing page, especially by country. But because search results are gated for logged-out users, it should not be treated like a classic public index strategy.

Implication:

- use `/jobs` and `/jobs/[country]` as SEO entry pages
- use supporting copy, FAQ, and country pages to capture intent
- do not rely on public crawlable result inventory as the growth engine

### 4. Authority gap versus big brands

For `ai resume builder` and `ats checker`, larger brands already combine:

- strong backlink profiles
- product usage signals
- branded search demand
- editorial content depth

RoleSharp will rank faster by winning adjacent, narrower, more truthful intents first.

## Internal Linking Strategy

This is one of the fastest wins.

Every major page should push users and crawlers through the same logic:

1. find or identify target role
2. choose a usable structure/template
3. tailor for the specific job
4. run ATS check
5. apply or save

### Required link paths

- `/resume-templates` -> `/resume-examples`
- `/resume-examples` -> `/build`
- `/ats-resume-checker` -> `/checker`
- `/jobs` -> `/build`
- `/students` -> `/resume-templates`
- role pages -> `/ats-resume-checker`
- blog posts -> one relevant product page, one relevant support page

## Biggest Ranking Risks

### 1. Publishing too much weak topical content

If RoleSharp starts publishing generic "resume tips" articles at scale, it will dilute authority rather than build it.

### 2. Chasing paid guest-post inventory

The sheets you shared are not the right growth path. Weak sites and spammy placement networks may add links, but not the right trust.

### 3. Overusing exact-match anchors

That is the fastest way to make outreach look synthetic.

### 4. Splitting link equity across duplicate-intent pages

If `/checker` and `/ats-resume-checker` are both pushed as the same SEO destination, ranking strength gets diluted.
