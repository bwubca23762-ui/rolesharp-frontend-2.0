# RoleSharp Traffic Outreach Shortlist

Written: 2026-07-08

This is a traffic-first outreach list for RoleSharp. The goal is not random backlinks. The goal is to publish on sites that already speak to:

- job seekers
- students
- resume improvers
- interview prep readers
- recruiters and HR teams adjacent to ATS / hiring workflows

## Rules

- Prefer topical relevance over raw DA/DR.
- Prefer editorial sites over "instant guest post" sellers.
- Prefer one useful contextual link over keyword-stuffed anchors.
- Do not use credential-heavy or spammy guest-post lists.
- Do not pitch product pages directly to strict editorial outlets.
- Best destination pages are useful resources, not hard-sell pages.

## Do Not Use

These are low-trust or poor-fit sources for RoleSharp:

- `Guest-Posting-Sheet.xlsx` from the `senddm.ai` redirect
  Reason: contains username/password-style fields and looks like mass placement inventory.
- `New-Guest-Post.xlsx`
  Reason: mostly quote/poetry/bio/general sites with weak topical fit for resumes/jobs/students.
- Instagram guest-post seller reels as a sourcing method
  Reason: repetitive low-quality engagement and no proof of editorial quality.

## Best RoleSharp Pages To Promote

Use these as the main destinations:

1. `/students`
   Best for student career, internship, campus, and early-career placements.
2. `/resume-templates`
   Best for resume formatting, ATS-safe structure, and resume basics.
3. `/resume-examples`
   Best for role-specific resume articles.
4. `/jobs`
   Best for job-search workflow and "find job then tailor resume" narratives.
5. `/build`
   Use carefully. Best on commercial-friendly or tool-focused pages, not strict editorial outlets.
6. `/blog/ats-friendly-resume-2026`
   Best for neutral editorial placements about ATS.
7. `/blog/job-search-faq`
   Best for general job-search advice placements.

## Anchor Strategy

Do not over-optimize anchors.

### Safe anchors
- `RoleSharp`
- `this ATS resume guide`
- `resume examples by role`
- `ATS-friendly resume templates`
- `student resume builder`
- `job search workflow`

### Avoid
- exact-match anchors like `best ai resume builder`
- repeated commercial anchors across many sites
- homepage links on strict editorial sites unless only bio links are allowed

## Pitch Framework

Use this pattern:

1. One-sentence credibility
2. One article idea
3. Why their audience needs it now
4. 3 bullet outline
5. Mention that the piece will be original and non-promotional

### Example

Subject: Guest post pitch: ATS-safe resume mistakes students still make

Hi [Name],

I work on RoleSharp, a resume and job-search product focused on ATS-safe resumes, student applications, and role-specific tailoring. I’d like to pitch a practical article for your audience:

`ATS-safe resume mistakes students still make before internship season`

Why it fits your readers:
- students often use generic templates that parse poorly
- they struggle to tailor one base resume across multiple roles
- most advice online is generic and not tied to real screening behavior

Proposed outline:
- the formatting mistakes that still hurt ATS parsing
- how students should tailor skills and project bullets to a job description
- a simple pre-application checklist before sending a resume

The article would be original, human-written, non-promotional, and tailored to your editorial style.

Best,
[Name]

## What To Do Next

1. Start with 5 Tier 1 pitches.
2. Use one article idea per site, not one mass pitch.
3. Point links to the page that best matches the article.
4. Keep commercial pages out of strict editorial submissions.
5. Track replies, approvals, drafts, and published links in a separate sheet.
